WRAP-UP STEPS
-------------
1. Expand the theory section again. Scroll to the "RLHF vs DPO at a Glance"
   box. Read it as a final summary.

2. Scroll back to the "Core Idea" box. Say: "You now understand every symbol:
   - R is the reward we learned in the Reward Model tab.
   - beta is the coefficient we tuned in the PPO tab.
   - KL is the divergence we explored in the KL Divergence tab.
   - pi is the policy, pi_ref is the frozen SFT model.
   - The whole expression says: maximise reward while staying close to the
   safe baseline."

3. Collapse the theory section. Switch to the PPO tab and leave the final
   experiment visible on screen as a visual summary.

4. Invite questions. If time permits, let a student run their own PPO
   experiment with a beta value of their choosing.
