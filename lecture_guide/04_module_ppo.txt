================================================================================
  ACT 3: PPO TAB (20 minutes)
  Tab: PPO (third tab)
  This is the longest and most interactive act. You will run 4 experiments.
================================================================================


COMPONENTS — LEFT PANEL
-----------------------
  1. Beta slider (KL Penalty Coefficient)
     Range: 0.00 to 1.00. Default: 0.20. Live readout next to label.
     THE most important parameter in RLHF.

  2. Learning Rate slider
     Range: 0.001 to 0.050. Default: 0.010.
     Controls policy update step size.

  3. Training Steps slider
     Range: 10 to 200. Default: 50.

  4. "Run PPO" button — animated full training run (~60ms per step).

  5. "Step" button — advances simulation by exactly one step.

  6. "Reset" button (red) — clears all history and charts.

  7. Stat pills (Step, Reward, KL Penalty, Net Objective)
     Updated live during training. Net Objective = Reward - beta * KL.

  8. Phase bar — changes colour and message based on training state:
     - Blue "Waiting": before training
     - Yellow "Optimising...": normal training
     - Red "High KL Penalty": KL penalty > 50% of reward
     - Green "Good Progress": net objective is high
     - Green "Training Complete": all steps finished


COMPONENTS — RIGHT PANEL
-------------------------
  9. Colour legend — Reward (blue), KL Penalty (red), Net Objective (green).

  10. "Training Dynamics" line chart (SVG)
      Three lines over training steps:
        Blue:  Reward (increasing, diminishing returns)
        Red:   Negative KL penalty (decreasing, growing magnitude)
        Green: Net Objective = Reward - beta*KL
      Zero line shown as dashed horizontal. End dots with numeric labels.

  11. "Reward vs KL Trade-off Frontier" scatter plot (SVG)
      X-axis: KL Divergence. Y-axis: Reward.
      Purple dots (opacity increases with step), connected by faint line.
      Current position highlighted with larger white-bordered dot.
      "Ideal: High reward, low KL" label in top-left.


TEACHING STEPS
--------------
1. Explain the controls briefly:
   - "Beta controls how much we penalise drifting from the SFT baseline."
   - "Learning rate controls step size."
   - "Run animates full training, Step goes one at a time, Reset starts over."

2. EXPERIMENT 1 — Baseline (beta=0.20, lr=0.010, steps=50):
   Keep defaults. Click "Run PPO". Narrate as animation runs:
   - "Blue reward line climbing... fast at first, then levels off."
   - "Red KL penalty line dropping below zero — penalty growing."
   - "Green net objective peaks then plateaus."
   Point to frontier plot: "Started at origin, moved right (more KL) and up
   (more reward). Ideal spot is top-left."
   Read final stats. Note phase bar colour.

3. Click "Reset".

4. EXPERIMENT 2 — No KL penalty (beta=0.00, lr=0.010, steps=100):
   Set beta to 0.00. Click "Run PPO". Narrate:
   - "With no KL penalty, reward climbs unchecked..."
   - "But look at the frontier — KL is exploding!"
   - "This is reward hacking. The model finds degenerate outputs that score
   high but are nonsensical."
   This answers the question from Act 1.

5. Click "Reset".

6. EXPERIMENT 3 — Very high KL penalty (beta=0.80, lr=0.010, steps=100):
   Set beta to 0.80. Click "Run PPO". Narrate:
   - "KL penalty dominates. Red line drops steeply."
   - "Net objective might go negative — penalty outweighs reward."
   - "Frontier: policy barely moves from origin. Over-regularisation."

7. Click "Reset".

8. EXPERIMENT 4 — Sweet spot (beta=0.15, lr=0.020, steps=80):
   Click "Run PPO". Narrate:
   - "Moderate beta, slightly higher learning rate..."
   - "Reward increases aggressively early, then the net objective peaks —
   that peak is roughly where we'd want to stop in practice."
   - "Frontier: fast vertical movement (reward gains), then mostly
   horizontal (KL growth, diminishing returns)."

9. Use "Step" button for 3-4 manual steps. For each step, read stat pills
   aloud and point to the new dot on the frontier. This slow-motion version
   helps students internalise per-step dynamics.

10. Summary: "Beta is the single most important hyperparameter in RLHF. Too
    low: reward hacking. Too high: no learning. These charts are the tools
    practitioners use to monitor real RLHF training runs."
